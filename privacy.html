Privacy

Handle personal information with care

This draft explains intended principles and must be reviewed and completed by qualified Nigerian privacy counsel before public launch.

Browse homesOur safety approach

Information that may be collected

Depending on which features are enabled, information may include contact details, account information, agent application details, identity documents, listing information, enquiries, inspection requests and complaints.

Why it is used

To operate accounts, review agents, moderate listings, respond to enquiries, investigate concerns, protect the service and meet applicable legal obligations.

Access and retention

Access should be limited by role. Identity documents should be private, accessed only when necessary and retained only for a defined period. Users should be told how to request access, correction or deletion where applicable.

Security and sharing

Do not share identity documents or contact details beyond authorised purposes. No online system can promise perfect security. Any service providers and cross-border data transfers must be reviewed before launch.

This is a working draft, not legal advice. Add the legal entity name, official contact, retention schedule, lawful basis and rights-request process after legal review.
