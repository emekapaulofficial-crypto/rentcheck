Before you pay

  • Visit the property and confirm the exact address.
  • Confirm the agent's identity and authority to rent the property.
  • Get a written breakdown of all charges and a signed agreement.
  • Avoid pressure, cash handovers without receipts and payments to unrelated third parties.
  • Keep messages, receipts and documents in a safe place.

“Reviewed” or “verified” only refers to the checks actually recorded. It is not a warranty of title, condition, availability or future conduct.
