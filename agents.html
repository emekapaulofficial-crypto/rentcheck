How it works

  1. Create your account. Use your email and a password. It takes one minute.
  2. Apply. Give your phone, WhatsApp, the areas you work in, and a photo or scan of an ID. Only our staff can see your ID.
  3. Get approved. We review applications ourselves and give you an Agent ID.
  4. Add apartments. Enter the details, upload as many photos as you have (up to 30), and give the owner's name and phone number. Owner details stay private.
  5. We verify and publish. Our team checks the agent, the property and the owner's authority. Approved homes go live with your name and WhatsApp.
  6. Close the deal. Renters contact you on WhatsApp. Commission is agreed in writing before you start.
