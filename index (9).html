The problem

Renting in Nigeria shouldn't feel like a gamble.

Every year, thousands of Nigerians lose money to fake agents, inflated "agreement fees," and apartments that don't match what was promised.

01

Agents who collect "inspection fees" or "agreement fees" then disappear, or show a property that was never actually available.

02

No way to compare listings side by side — renters often deal with a single agent and have nothing to check the offer against.

03

Hidden issues — flooding, noise, disputed ownership — that only surface after money has changed hands.
