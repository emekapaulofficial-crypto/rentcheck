Tell the RentCheck NG team about a suspicious listing, misleading information, unexpected fees or an agent interaction that concerns you. Reports are reviewed; a report alone does not prove wrongdoing.

Questions renters ask

Before you make a move.

Does a listing badge guarantee a property?

No. A badge should describe the specific checks completed and when. Always view the property and confirm terms before paying.

Are all listed fees included?

The platform aims to show all charges provided and confirmed by the owner or agent. Ask for a written total move-in cost before committing.

Can I browse without an account?

Yes. Browsing public listings should not require an account. Some future features, such as saved homes, may require sign-in.

How do I become an agent?

Submit an application. The team must review it before the applicant can publish approved listings as a RentCheck NG agent.
