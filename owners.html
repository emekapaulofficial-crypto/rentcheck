For owners

List a property with clear information

Accurate listings help renters understand the home, fees and next steps before they travel.

Browse homesOur safety approach
  1. Prepare accurate details about the location, type, bedrooms and availability.
  2. Use current photos of the actual property, not illustrative images.
  3. Disclose rent and every known fee clearly.
  4. Be ready to confirm your authority to rent or appoint an agent.
  5. Respond promptly and correct stale details.

The current owner enquiry on the homepage may prepare a WhatsApp message rather than store a listing. Do not assume submission means the property is published.
