About Calvora

Property. Trust. Opportunity.

Property. Trust. Opportunity.

Finding a home or a plot of land in Nigeria should not come with fear. Too many people have paid for a property that did not exist, was already taken, or turned out to cost far more than they were told.

Calvora Property Solutions was set up to change that. We bring together homes for rent, homes for sale and land for sale from registered agents, and we check each property before it is called verified.

We are starting in Ado-Ekiti and will open new cities only when the first one is working well for customers, agents and landlords.

What we stand for

  • Honesty. No fake listings, no misleading prices, no hidden fees.
  • Proof. We say “verified” only when the checks are on record.
  • Accountability. Every agent has an ID you can check, and every complaint gets a reference number.
  • Privacy. We keep customer and owner information private.

What we do

For customers

Search three clear catalogues, see the full cost of a property, book an inspection and deal with an agent whose record you can see.

For landlords and owners

Reach people looking for property without answering every enquiry yourself, with your permission recorded before anything goes live.

For agents

A professional platform, real customer enquiries, a personal dashboard and a badge that grows with your honest track record.

How we verify

  1. Agent identity checked

    We confirm who the agent is before they can list.

  2. Property viewed

    Our team sees the property, not just photos of it.

  3. Owner authority checked

    We confirm the owner allows it to be advertised.

  4. Details confirmed

    Price, fees and availability are recently confirmed with the owner.

We re-confirm availability regularly. A listing that has not been re-confirmed within 30 days is labelled “Availability Confirmation Pending”.
