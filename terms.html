Listing information

Listings may change or become unavailable. Users must independently confirm details, fees, authority to rent and property condition before entering an agreement.

Agent conduct

Agents must submit accurate information, use genuine property photos, disclose all known fees, respect customer privacy and not impersonate owners or pressure customers to pay unrelated accounts.

Platform role

Prime Estate & Properties provides discovery, review and communication tools. Unless a separate written service says otherwise, it is not the landlord, does not guarantee title or condition and does not hold rental deposits.

Reports and moderation

Users may report suspicious content. The platform may request information, hide listings or suspend accounts while concerns are reviewed.
