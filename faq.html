FAQs

Straight answers for renters and agents

A quick guide to what RentCheck NG does and what you should still confirm yourself.

Browse homesOur safety approach

Does RentCheck guarantee every property?

No. We aim to document review steps and be transparent about their limits. You should independently inspect and confirm all material details.

Does a listing mean the property is still available?

Not necessarily. Availability can change. Contact the agent before travelling or paying.

What costs should I ask about?

Annual rent, agency fee, caution deposit, legal fee, service charges, utilities and any other move-in cost.

How do I become an agent?

Use the agent portal to create an account and submit the required application. Approval is not automatic.

How do I report suspicious activity?

Use the report page and include the listing, contact details, dates and evidence you can safely share.

Does RentCheck collect rent or deposits?

Do not pay RentCheck unless an explicitly documented, approved service and payment process is provided. The current pilot is not designed to hold rent or deposits.
