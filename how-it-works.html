How it works

Search, ask, inspect, then decide

RentCheck NG is designed to help you compare options without rushing into a payment.

Browse homesOur safety approach
  1. Search homes. Start with location, property type and budget.
  2. Compare known costs. Ask for rent, agency, caution, legal and other charges in writing.
  3. Contact the responsible agent. Confirm that the property is still available and that the agent is authorised to arrange a viewing.
  4. Inspect in person where possible. Check the actual home, access, utilities and surrounding area.
  5. Verify before paying. Confirm ownership or authority, use a traceable payment method and keep receipts and agreements.
  6. Report concerns. If something seems suspicious, use the report page and keep relevant evidence.
Never send money solely because a listing appears on a website. Do not let urgency replace independent checks.
